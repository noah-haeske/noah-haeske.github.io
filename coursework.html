The program

What I am building toward.

I arrived at UF with 47 credits of dual enrollment from Valencia College, which put the calculus and general chemistry sequences behind me before my first semester started. That bought time, and I spent it on research rather than on a lighter schedule: I registered for Engineering Research in Fall 2025, my first term on campus, and have carried it every semester since.

The mechanics and thermal sciences core is where the coursework starts to overlap with the lab. I am taking Statics, Materials, Thermodynamics, and Numerical Methods this semester, which means I sized a latent heat buffer in London over the summer before I had formally taken thermodynamics. The class has since been largely an explanation of why the things I found empirically had to be true. That is a decent argument for doing the research early rather than waiting to be qualified for it.
