I am a mechanical engineering student at the University of Florida, working between microfluidics and instrumentation. At UF's Interdisciplinary Microsystems Group I fabricate and test devices that isolate circulating tumor cells from blood. Last summer at Imperial College London I built a point-of-care diagnostic cassette that utilized exothermic chemical reactions and polymer rupture barriers to passively amplify DNA. I also lead test vehicles for Florida Rocket Lab.
